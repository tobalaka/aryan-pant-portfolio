# HeXD Lab: underwater acoustics and a VR sound simulation

Summer 2026 research internship at the Human-centered eXtended Reality (HeXD) Lab at the University of Texas at Dallas.

- Researched underwater acoustics with UT Dallas PhD students.
- Produced a research paper and a poster.
- Built a VR sound simulation related to underwater sound.
- Submitted the poster to an IEEE AR/VR contest. The submission is not an award or publication.

This work led me to learn about human-generated ocean noise, which later shaped the environmental theme of my game [TRASH](../trash).
